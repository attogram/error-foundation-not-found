We are forming a non-profit foundation, based in Amsterdam.

We are inspired by the playful and provocative 1960s Provo movement and the open ethos of the early Internet Hacker culture.

The Open Error Foundation is an AI-native media studio and civic research lab. We produce open music, podcasts, videos, and academic papers—using our own creative output as a live probe to surface, document, and publish systemic errors across human, institutional, and machine workflows.
